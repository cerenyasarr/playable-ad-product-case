## 01. Identifying New Market Opportunities
*Choosing a game company that could benefit from Playable Factory's services and designing a playable ad concept for it.*

### 🎯 Task 1.1 — Company Selection: Rotate Lab — Cube Land
I chose Rotate Lab, specifically its game **Cube Land**. The game has reached **1M+ downloads** and **$600K+ in revenue**; this indicates the company is on the rise and in a growth phase.

**Why Rotate Lab?**
- Being based in Türkiye provides an advantage in terms of communication, rapid testing processes, and creative feedback loops. 
- As a smaller company, their capabilities on the playable side may be limited, and producing playables quickly may be challenging for them.
- Cube Land's current playable demonstrates the mechanics of shooting at cubes and clearing objects. Playable Factory could help produce and test different creative variations of this core mechanic faster (e.g., more curved shots or stronger animations to enhance the sense of clearing).
- Through different creative variations, the game's IPM (Installs Per Mille) and CVR (Conversion Rate) can be increased, helping reduce Rotate Lab's CPI.

### 🧩 Task 1.2 — Playable Ad Concept: “Blast & Clean”
A playable ad concept designed for Rotate Lab's Cube Land — letting users experience the cube-blasting and full object-clearing loop in a short time, increasing satisfaction and install intent.

- **Objective:** Let users experience Cube Land's cube-blasting loop in a short time.
- **Target Audience:** Mobile players aged 13–65 who enjoy casual and block puzzle games.
- **Main Message:** *“Send the shooters in the right order, blast the cubes, and clear the object completely!”*
- **Core Metrics:** IPM ↑, CVR ↑, CPI ↓

### 🎬 Blast & Clean — Scene-by-Scene Flow
*Five scenes designed to hook within the first 3 seconds and convert.*

1. **Initial Setup & Camera Hook (0-3 sec):** As soon as the playable opens, the camera quickly rotates around the target object and settles into the ideal shooting angle. This dynamic camera movement serves as a critical “Hook”.
2. **Tutorial & Call to Action (Guidance):** A prominent “TAP TO PLAY” text appears. The first correct cube the player needs to select is highlighted with a glowing effect, and a hand animation guides the user to tap.
3. **Gameplay & Satisfying Effects:** As the player launches the cubes, matching areas begin to clear. Visual effects that create the feeling of haptic feedback, light screen shakes, and satisfying particle animations are activated.
4. **Win Screen (Celebration):** The area is successfully cleared. Celebration messages (“Well Done!”) appear alongside a dynamic confetti explosion. 
5. **End Card (CTA):** The screen becomes blurred. The Cube Land logo is displayed at the top, while a large pulsing “INSTALL NOW” button is placed in the center.

## 02. Proposing Product Improvement
*A one-page proposal presentation to promote a chosen Playable Factory product.*

### 🚀 Task 2 — Product Improvement Proposal: FLEX
**Turn Winning Video Creatives into Interactive Playables in Minutes**
*Evolving Flex from a manual editing tool into an automated workflow.*

**Why Flex?**
Flex enables teams to turn existing video creatives into interactive playable ads quickly. It is especially valuable for high-performing video creatives that have already proven their appeal through CTR and CVR results.

**The Opportunity:**
I propose evolving Flex from a manual editing tool into an **automated workflow** that identifies winning video creatives and turns them into interactive Flex Playables, without human intervention in the loop.

**How It Works (4-Step Auto-Flex Workflow):**
1. **Launch:** Video creatives are launched and tested on ad networks.
2. **Monitor:** Auto-Flex monitors CTR and CVR performance through network APIs.
3. **Augment:** When a creative exceeds the defined threshold, AI automatically adds interaction points, tap areas, and guiding hand icons.
4. **Push:** The playable is generated and pushed back to the ad network within minutes.

**Why It Matters:**
- **Speed:** Turn a winning video into a playable within minutes.
- **Scalability:** Test hundreds of video variations without creating an engineering bottleneck.
- **Performance:** Complex scenes (like 10,000-cube physics) can be delivered as video-based playables without lag.
- **Capture the hype:** Automatically produce playables for overnight hits while the team is offline.

## 03. Evaluating Playable Factory's Playable
*Evaluate the All in Hole playable ad built with Playable Studio. Identify the three most critical improvements.*

### 🔍 Task 3.1 — Game Mechanics: All in Hole
*The playable turns All in Hole's core "collect and grow" loop into a short and fast-paced experience.*

**What the Current Playable Already Does Well (Strengths):**
1. **Strong Opening Hook:** The opening message, "Gulp them all!", makes the objective immediately understandable.
2. **Immediate Interaction:** Offering two character options at the beginning immediately invites the user to interact.
3. **Pace & Pressure:** The time limit, progress bar, and red screen effect create urgency.
4. **Accelerated Satisfaction:** The hole grows faster in the playable compared to the actual game.

### 🛠 3 Critical Changes to Implement

**01. Tutorial & First-Second Engagement**
*Making the first few seconds self-explanatory; the tutorial is the hook.*
- **Tutorial Addition:** Add a joystick showing an infinity symbol to guide the player’s first move. The first interaction should feel satisfying.
- **Character Selection Feedback:** When selecting one of the two characters, add a scale-down or scale-up effect so the user understands it is clickable.

**02. Progression & Satisfying Feedback**
*Breaking big structures into a satisfying waterfall of collectible objects.*
- **Physics Waterfall:** Huge stacked structures can be broken into smaller pieces and collected smoothly into the hole with a realistic physics animation.
- **Level Indicator & Progress Circle:** Place a level indicator underneath the hole or a circle around it to communicate progress.
- **Effects Layer:** Add absorption particles and screen shakes to enhance the feeling of collection.

**03. CTA & Power-Up Usage**
*Turning the end-of-playable moment into an unmissable install trigger.*
- **CTA Placement:** Reveal the progress bars next to the CTA and place the game icon or “Download Now” in the other corner. If the user does not tap the close button, redirect to the app store. *(Do NOT use fake close buttons as networks reject them).*
- **Power-Up as CTA Trigger:** Let users tap on power-ups (like a Super Magnet) to be redirected to the store, creating a strong incentive to download.

## 💡 Conclusion: Three Principles, One Way of Thinking
1. **The First 3 Seconds Are Decisive:** Camera movement, immediate interaction, and clear messaging matter most.
2. **Tutorials Materially Improve Conversion:** Users who understand what to do within seconds are far more likely to reach the install moment.
3. **Satisfaction Makes It Persuasive:** Satisfying feedback, animation, physics, particles, and screen shake are what turn a functional playable into a genuinely persuasive one.
